How to remove an object

  1. Upload the image you want to clean up.
  2. Use Rectangle to box the object, or Brush to paint over it. Use Eraser to refine brush marks.
  3. Include a little surrounding area so the model can fill it naturally.
  4. Review the preview and download the result.

Use Undo or Redo to adjust your marks before processing. Brush works best for irregular shapes.
