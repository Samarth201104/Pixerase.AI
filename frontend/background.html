How background removal works

  1. Upload a photo with a clear subject.
  2. Choose Remove background for a transparent PNG.
  3. To replace the scene, select a template or upload your own, then choose Apply background.
  4. Preview and download your result.

For cleaner edges, use a well-lit image where the subject stands apart from the background.
